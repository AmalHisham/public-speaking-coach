# **Public Speaking Coach**

## **Product Requirements Document (PRD) v2.0**

Version: 2.0  
Status: Approved for MVP

---

# **1\. Product Vision**

Public Speaking Coach helps people improve their presentation and communication skills through objective analytics and actionable coaching.

The platform analyzes speech and presentation behavior using audio and video signals, converts them into measurable metrics, and provides personalized improvement recommendations.

The product focuses on measurable communication behaviors rather than subjective judgments.

---

# **2\. Problem Statement**

People often struggle to improve their speaking skills because feedback is:

* Subjective  
* Inconsistent  
* Difficult to measure  
* Expensive to obtain  
* Hard to track over time

Most speakers do not know:

* Whether they speak too fast or too slowly  
* How often they use filler words  
* Whether they pause effectively  
* Whether they maintain stable posture  
* Whether they remain engaged with their audience  
* Whether they are improving over time

Without objective feedback, improvement becomes difficult.

# **4\. Value Proposition**

Users can practice a presentation and immediately receive:

* Speaking Pace Analysis  
* Filler Usage Analysis  
* Pause Quality Analysis  
* Camera Engagement Analysis  
* Posture Stability Analysis  
* AI Coaching Feedback

The platform provides measurable insights instead of subjective opinions.

---

# **5\. Product Principles**

## **Principle 1**

Measure only what can be objectively calculated.

---

## **Principle 2**

Every metric must be explainable.

---

## **Principle 3**

Metrics are generated by formulas, not AI.

---

## **Principle 4**

AI interprets metrics but does not create metrics.

---

## **Principle 5**

Historical improvement is more valuable than a single session score.

# **8\. Explicit Non-Goals**

The MVP will NOT attempt to measure:

* Confidence  
* Charisma  
* Leadership Presence  
* Executive Presence  
* Persuasiveness  
* Trustworthiness  
* Audience Engagement  
* Passion  
* Authenticity

Reason:

These concepts cannot be measured objectively.

---

# **9\. Success Metrics**

## **User Success**

Users understand:

* Speaking pace  
* Filler usage  
* Pause quality  
* Camera engagement  
* Posture stability

after every session.

---

## **Product Success**

Users complete:

* At least 3 sessions per week

and show measurable improvement over time.

---

## **Technical Success**

System provides:

* Reliable metric calculation  
* Session persistence  
* Report generation  
* Historical tracking

without requiring video uploads.

---

# **10\. MVP Release Criteria**

The MVP is considered complete when users can:

✓ Create an account

✓ Log in

✓ Start a session

✓ End a session

✓ Receive speech analytics

✓ Receive video analytics

✓ Receive AI feedback

✓ View session history

✓ Track improvement over time

---

# **11\. Future Roadmap (Out of Scope)**

Future versions may include:

* Real-time coaching  
* Interview-specific coaching  
* Team dashboards  
* Coach accounts  
* Presentation slide analysis  
* Video uploads  
* Mobile applications  
* Enterprise analytics

These features are explicitly excluded from MVP.

---

# **12\. Product Positioning**

Public Speaking Coach is a presentation analytics platform that uses objective communication metrics and AI-powered coaching to help users become better speakers.

The platform is not an AI judge.

The platform is an analytics and improvement tool.
